# Hot Wash

Do this within the hour, while it's fresh and before anyone leaves the room.

The hot wash isn't the after-action report. It's the three questions you ask before memory,and a double bourbon after a rough starts editing what happened.

## Three questions

**What worked?** Three things. Specific ones.

**What didn't?** Three things. Honest ones.

**Who owns it?** A name on every fix.
