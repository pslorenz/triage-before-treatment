# Triage

Tag before you treat. Every affected system gets one category, assigned by the incident commander, before anyone starts fixing things.

These categories are borrowed from START, the mass-casualty triage method used by EMS and fire services. Real START sorts patients by breathing, circulation, and mental status in under a minute each. This kit borrows the speed and the colors, not the clinical criteria.

## The four categories

| Category | Color | Meaning for an asset |
|---|---|---|
| **Expectant** | Black | Accept the loss. Move on. |
| **Immediate** | Red | Operations stopped |
| **Delayed** | Yellow | Degraded, still running |
| **Minor** | Green | Walking wounded |

Expectant is the hardest tag. If nobody in your organization is allowed to say "we're letting this one go," everything stays red forever, and two admins try to save five systems at once.
