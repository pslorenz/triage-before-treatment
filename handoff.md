# The Handoff

SBAR goes up. MIST goes across: to the retainer firm, the carrier's forensics team, your MSP/MSSP, or the next shift. They need this before they touch anything.

Fill it in as you go, not at the end.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | **Mechanism** | How it got in, as far as you know |
| **I** | **Injuries** | What's down, damaged, or exposed |
| **S** | **Signs** | What you're seeing right now |
| **T** | **Treatment given** | Everything you already changed |

## Why T matters most

Treatment given is the line teams can't answer. Every password reset, reimage, firewall block, and restore changed the evidence. If nobody wrote them down, the people taking over have to reconstruct what you did before they can figure out what the attacker did. They bill by the hour for that.
